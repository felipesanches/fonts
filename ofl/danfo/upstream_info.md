## Summary

Danfo was designed by Afrotype (Seyi Olusanya, Eyiyemi Adegbite, David Udoh) and Mirko Velimirović. It was published as a variable font with a custom ELSH axis (0–100) covering Latin, Latin Extended, and Vietnamese subsets. The font file was sourced from `fonts/variable/Danfo[ELSH].ttf`. A minisite is available at https://www.afrotype.com/danfo.
