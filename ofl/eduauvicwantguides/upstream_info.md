## Summary

Edu AU VIC WA NT Guides (display name: Edu Australia VIC WA NT Hand Guides) was designed by Tina Anderson and Corey Anderson. It was published as a variable font with a wght axis (400–700) covering Latin and Latin Extended subsets. The font file was sourced from `fonts/variable/EduAUVICWANTGuides[wght].ttf`. This is one of several Australian state school handwriting fonts in the same upstream repository.
