## Summary

Edu AU VIC WA NT Hand (display name: Edu Australia VIC WA NT Hand) was designed by Tina Anderson and Corey Anderson. It was published as a variable font with a wght axis (400–700) covering Latin and Latin Extended subsets. The font file was sourced from `fonts/variable/EduAUVICWANTHand[wght].ttf`. This is the base handwriting style in the VIC WA NT Australian state school handwriting font collection.
