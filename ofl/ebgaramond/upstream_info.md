## Summary

EB Garamond was originally designed by Georg Duffner and later maintained by Octavio Pardo. It was published as variable fonts with a wght axis (400–800) in both upright and italic styles, covering Cyrillic, Cyrillic Extended, Greek, Greek Extended, Latin, Latin Extended, and Vietnamese subsets. A specimen minisite is available at https://googlefonts.github.io/ebgaramond-specimen/.
