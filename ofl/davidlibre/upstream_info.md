## Summary

David Libre was designed by Meir Sadan, based on work by Monotype Imaging and SIL International. It was published in three weights (Regular 400, Medium 500, Bold 700) covering the Hebrew script alongside Latin, Latin Extended, Vietnamese, Math, and Symbols subsets. Font files were sourced from `fonts/ttf/`. Primary script is Hebrew.
