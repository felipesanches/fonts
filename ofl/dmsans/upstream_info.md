## Summary

DM Sans was designed by Colophon Foundry and hosted under the googlefonts GitHub organization. It was published as variable fonts with opsz (9–40) and wght (100–1000) axes in both upright and italic styles, covering Latin and Latin Extended subsets. Font files were sourced from `Sans/fonts/variable/`.
