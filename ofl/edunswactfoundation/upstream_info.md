## Summary

Edu NSW ACT Foundation was designed by Tina Anderson and Corey Anderson. It was published as a variable font with a wght axis (400–700) covering Latin subsets. The font file was sourced from `NSW-ACT-School-Fonts/fonts/variable/EduNSWACTFoundation[wght].ttf`. Part of a suite of Australian state school handwriting fonts hosted in the MezMerrit/AU-School-Handwriting-Fonts repository.
