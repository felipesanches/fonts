## Summary

Datatype was designed by Frank Tisellano. It was published as a variable font with wdth (50–150) and wght (100–900) axes covering Latin and Latin Extended subsets. The font file was sourced from `fonts/variable/Datatype[wdth,wght].ttf`. A minisite is available at https://franktisellano.github.io/datatype.
